Delete Your Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your DiploMate account and data, including how to do so without installing or opening the app.

What deletion removes

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile information (name, email, photo), your chosen speciality, your full study history (every question and answer card you reviewed and how you rated your recall), your bookmarks, and any error reports you submitted.

This action is irreversible

Once your account is deleted, it cannot be restored. Your study history and progress cannot be recovered. Please make sure you want to proceed before requesting deletion.

How to delete your account

There are two ways to request deletion:

Option 1: In the app

Open DiploMate, go to Account, then select Delete account, and confirm the prompt. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2: By email, without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to support@diplomate.app from the same Google account address you used to sign in, with the subject line "Delete my account". Include the email address of the Google account in the body of your message so we can locate your data.

Response time

Deletion requests submitted by email are honored within 30 days of receipt. In-app deletion takes effect immediately.

Questions

If you have questions before deleting your account, contact support@diplomate.app. See also our Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and why.
