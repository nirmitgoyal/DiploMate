Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-05

DiploMate is a study app for postgraduate medical exam preparation. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, where it is stored, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your account email address, name, and profile photo. As you use the app we also store:

  • The speciality you choose to study (for example, Anaesthesia).
  • Your study events: which question and answer cards you reviewed, how you rated your own recall of each one, and when.
  • Bookmarks you save on individual questions.
  • Error reports you choose to submit against specific content, along with any message you type.

Why we collect it

We use this information to sync your study progress across your devices, to compute study statistics such as streaks and cards reviewed, and to identify content that needs correction or improvement. We do not use your data for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a Heroku Postgres database hosted in a United States region. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not show ads in the app.

Crash and error reporting

We use Sentry to capture app crash reports so we can fix bugs. Crash payloads never include the text of any question or answer you were viewing, and never include your authentication tokens. Crash reports are limited to technical details about the crash itself, such as the device type, app version, and the error stack trace.

How long we keep your data

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. Once you delete your account, your data is removed as described below.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data in either of two ways:

  • In the app: go to Account, then Delete account, and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, study events, bookmarks, and speciality choice.
  • On the web: visit /account/delete for instructions on requesting deletion without opening the app.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@diplomate.app.
