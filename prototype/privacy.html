Privacy Policy

Effective 4 July 2026 · Last updated 4 July 2026

1 Who we are

DiploMate is a study application for candidates preparing for the Diplomate of National Board (DNB) theory examinations. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the DiploMate mobile app and to the website at diplomate.app (together, the “Service”).

The Service is operated by DiploMate LLP (“we”, “us”), based in India. To reach us about privacy, email privacy@diplomate.app.

2 Information we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, Google profile picture, and Google account identifier. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Study information

To run your recall queue we store the exam speciality you choose, which answers you view and reveal, the confidence rating you give each one (Unable to recall / Hard / Good), the times of those reviews, your bookmarks, and your daily reminder setting. Your recall history is kept as an append-only log so your spaced-repetition schedule survives app updates.

Purchase information

DiploMate is free during the current beta. When paid access is enabled, or where beta access is granted manually, we store the payment reference you send us (for example, a UPI transaction reference) and your entitlement status. We do not store your card, bank, or UPI credentials.

Technical information

We automatically collect device model and operating system, app version, IP address, and diagnostic and log data. On diplomate.app we also use cookies and similar technologies (see section 3).

3 Cookies and similar technologies

By accessing or using the Service, you consent to our use of all cookies and similar technologies — including local storage, device identifiers, and SDK storage — for every purpose described in this policy, including analytics and performance measurement, for as long as you use the Service. We do not display a separate cookie banner or ask for cookie choices in the Service; your continued use is your consent.

If you do not agree, do not use the Service. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser or device settings, and you can reset your device advertising identifier — but some parts of the Service may not work correctly if you do.

4 How we use your information

  • To provide the study loop — showing answers, recording your recall ratings, and scheduling reviews.
  • To sync your progress across your devices through your account.
  • To send the daily reminder you set, and important service messages.
  • To grant and manage access to paid content and honour your purchases.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with law and to protect the rights and safety of our users and of us.

5 On what basis we process your information

We process your information with your consent, given when you use the Service, and as necessary to provide the Service you have asked for and to meet our legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (section 8); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

6 Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, to run the Service:

  • Google — for sign-in and authentication.
  • Heroku (Salesforce) — application hosting and the database that stores your account and study data.
  • Vercel — hosting for the diplomate.app website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to enforce our terms, prevent fraud, or protect safety. If the Service is transferred to another owner, information may be transferred with it under this policy.

7 Where your information is stored

Our hosting and database run on servers located in the United States. If you use the Service from India or elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

8 Your choices and rights

You can view and change your speciality and reminder in the app, and update your name and email through your Google account. You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from Account → Delete account, or by emailing privacy@diplomate.app. Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal and study data within 30 days, except anything we must keep to meet a legal, tax, or accounting obligation.

9 How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Your recall log is retained while your account exists so your review schedule stays intact. After account deletion we remove it as described in section 8.

10 Security

Access to paid content and to your data is enforced on our servers, and connections use encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to limit who can access it.

11 Children

The Service is intended for medical graduates preparing for postgraduate examinations and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the Service, contact us and we will delete the information.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will post the new version here and update the date above; significant changes may also be notified in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13 Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: privacy@diplomate.app.
